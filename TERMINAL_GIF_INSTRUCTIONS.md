### Recommended Settings:
- **Theme**: Dark (matches your profile)
- **Font**: Fira Code or JetBrains Mono
- **Speed**: Medium typing speed
- **Window Style**: macOS or minimal

1. Download the GIF
2. Rename it to `about_renan.gif`
3. Place it in `daria-stanilevici/assets/` folder
